Farmer's Guide to Using Our Website

Welcome

Welcome to our agricultural website. This guide will help you understand how to add your products and transport vehicles to our platform. Follow the instructions below to get started.

Adding Your Products

  1. Navigate to the Products section from the main menu.
  2. Click on the "Add Product" button.
  3. Fill in the product details including name, description, price, and an image URL.
  4. Click "Submit" to add your product to the list.

Adding Your Transport Vehicles

  1. Navigate to the Transport Vehicles section from the main menu.
  2. Click on the "Add Vehicle" button.
  3. Fill in the vehicle details including name, description, price, and an image URL.
  4. Click "Submit" to add your vehicle to the list.

Editing Your Listings

If you need to make changes to your existing listings, follow these steps:

  1. Go to the My Listings section from the main menu.
  2. Find the product or vehicle you want to edit and click on the "Edit" button.
  3. Update the necessary details and click "Save" to apply the changes.

Getting Help

If you encounter any issues or need further assistance, please reach out to our support team through the Contact Us page. We are here to help you!
